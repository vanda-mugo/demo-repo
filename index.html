Scenario 1 — Field Sales: Order to Cash

10 Checkpoints
📋Checkpoint 1 — Order Creation
  • Customer must exist — order cannot be created for an unknown customer ID
  • Walk-in customer — if generic "WALK-IN" account, agent must supply actual customer name (mandatory, rejects blank/whitespace)
  • At least one line item — order with zero items cannot be saved
  • Payment type rules validated — FULL requires bank account; PARTIAL requires bank account + amount > 0; CREDIT needs no bank account
  • Stock pre-check — system checks available stock at selected warehouse for every line item; returns per-product error if short
✅Checkpoint 2 — Approval & Debtor Report
  • Customer Debtor Report accessible to approver — shows credit limit, outstanding balance, available credit, over-limit flag
  • Aging buckets displayed — Current, 1–30 days, 31–60 days, 61–90 days, Over 90 days
  • Unpaid Invoice List shown — invoice number, date, due date, total, paid, balance, days overdue
  • Status gate — only SUBMITTED orders can be approved or rejected; confirmed/completed orders blocked
  • Rejection requires a reason — rejection note mandatory and stored against the order
🚚Checkpoint 3 — Dispatch Order Creation
  • Sales order must be CONFIRMED — DRAFT, SUBMITTED, or processing orders rejected
  • Only 50 kg cylinder products — other SKUs named and directed to milk-run process
  • No duplicate active dispatch — existing active dispatch blocks second creation, shows existing dispatch number
  • Stock availability hard-check — warehouse stock re-checked at dispatch creation
  • Stock reservations created automatically — ring-fenced, cannot allocate to other orders
🚐Checkpoint 4 — Trip Creation & Order Assignment
  • Vehicle must be active — inactive/off-road/decommissioned vehicles rejected
  • Vehicle cylinder capacity enforced — total expected count cannot exceed capacity
  • Order–warehouse match — dispatch order source warehouse must match trip warehouse
  • No double-booking — dispatch already assigned to different trip cannot be added to second trip
  • Orders with scanned cylinders cannot be removed — must un-scan first
  • Loading must start before scanning — cylinders only scanned while trip in LOADING status
📱Checkpoint 5 — Cylinder Scanning onto Trip
  • Trip must be in LOADING — scanning locked outside loading phase
  • Cylinder must exist — scanned QR/serial number must be registered in system
  • Cylinder must be IN_STOCK — IN_TRANSIT, SOLD, SCRAPPED, or non-stock statuses rejected
  • Cylinder must be FULL — EMPTY or PARTIAL scan produces error showing actual fill status
  • Cylinder must be at trip's warehouse — cross-warehouse scanning blocked
  • No duplicate scanning — same cylinder twice onto same trip blocked
  • Vehicle capacity not exceeded — scanning beyond configured limit blocked
  • After successful scan, cylinder status immediately changes to IN_TRANSIT; removal reverts to IN_STOCK
🛣️Checkpoint 6 — Trip Departure (IN_TRANSIT)
  • All expected cylinders must be scanned — scanned count must equal expected count before departure
  • Loading revert protection — revert to CREATED blocked if any cylinders scanned
  • Stock movements created at departure — SALES_DELIVERY stock-out records created permanently
🎯Checkpoint 7 — Cylinder Assignment to Customer Orders
  • Trip must be IN_TRANSIT or DELIVERING — assignment blocked for other statuses
  • Cylinder cannot be double-assigned — already assigned to one order blocked from another
  • Product must match order — cylinder product must correspond to dispatch order item
  • Maximum cylinder count enforced — cannot assign more than ordered quantity
  • Stock reservations fulfilled per cylinder — each assignment auto-fulfils one reservation unit
📦Checkpoint 8 — Delivery Recording & Empty Cylinder Collection
  • Assigned cylinders marked DELIVERED and status changed to SOLD with customer and sale date recorded
  • Empty return: cylinder must be registered — serial number must exist in system
  • Empty return: cylinder must be at customer's premises (status SOLD) — IN_STOCK, IN_TRANSIT, SCRAPPED, DEFECTIVE rejected
  • Empty return: cylinder must belong to THIS customer — cross-customer collection blocked
  • Partial empties supported — flagged as PARTIAL with gross weight recorded; tracked separately
💳Checkpoint 9 — Checkout & Payment
  • Only DRAFT orders can be checked out directly — CONFIRMED/PROCESSING must go through dispatch flow
  • Bank account must be active and belong to the tenant — inactive/deleted accounts rejected
  • Payment splits validation — minimum 2 entries, each needs bank account + amount > 0, sum must equal total (±0.01)
  • Partial payment: amount required and must be greater than zero
  • Credit sale: no bank account required; invoice created in SENT status with 30-day due date
  • Over-payment blocked — payment larger than outstanding balance rejected
  • Automatic accounting entries posted — DR COGS/CR Inventory; DR AR/CR Revenue; DR Bank/CR AR
🏁Checkpoint 10 — Trip Completion
  • Trip-linked dispatch orders must complete via trip — direct completion blocked
  • Completion finalises all order statuses — sales orders → COMPLETED, dispatch orders → COMPLETED, TripCylinder records finalised

Scenario 2 — Bulk Gas Purchase Flow

3 Checkpoints
📝Checkpoint 1 — Purchase Order Lifecycle
  • DRAFT → SUBMITTED: PO cannot be submitted without line items and valid supplier
  • SUBMITTED → APPROVED: requires purchases.orders.approve permission
  • SUBMITTED → REJECTED: rejection reason recorded, PO returns for revision
  • APPROVED → SENT: PO sent to supplier; cannot be edited after this point
  • Edit restriction — PO only updatable in DRAFT status
  • Cancel restriction — only permitted for orders not yet received
  • Create-and-approve shortcut — single-step operation bypasses draft-submit workflow
📥Checkpoint 2 — Bulk Gas Receipt (GRN)
  • Must reference valid Purchase Order — linked to existing PO belonging to matching supplier
  • PO must be in receivable state — cancelled or already-closed PO cannot generate receipts
  • Tank must be active — gas only receipted into ACTIVE tank; inactive/decommissioned rejected
  • Quantity (kg) and unit cost per kg both mandatory fields
  • Tank level before captured automatically for before/after audit trail
  • Draft-only edits — receipt only editable in DRAFT status
✅Checkpoint 3 — Receipt Confirmation
  • Must be in DRAFT to confirm — already-confirmed or cancelled receipts cannot be re-confirmed
  • Tank level updated automatically — quantity added to current level; tank-level-after recorded
  • Accounting entry created — DR Inventory / CR Accounts Payable
  • Cancel restriction after confirmation — confirmed receipts cannot be cancelled; manual adjustment required

Scenario 3 — 50 kg Cylinder Registration

3 Checkpoints
🏷️Checkpoint 1 — Product Prerequisite
  • Idempotent product creation — "ensure-product" call finds or creates default 50 kg cylinder product (SKU: CYL-50KG); no duplicates on repeat calls
  • Category auto-created — "Gas Cylinders" category created if missing
  • Unit auto-created — "Pieces (PCS)" unit created if missing
🔢Checkpoint 2 — Cylinder Registration
  • Product must exist — 50 kg cylinder product must be present before registration
  • Serial number uniqueness — custom serial checked against tenant inventory; duplicates rejected with conflict error
  • Auto-generated serial format — CYL-50-YYYYMM-NNNN (e.g. CYL-50-202604-0023), incrementing from highest existing number in current month
  • Warehouse assignment — every cylinder must be assigned to a warehouse at registration
  • Fill status at registration — can be EMPTY (default) or FULL; affects filling/dispatch eligibility
  • QR code returned immediately — printable QR string returned as permanent identity link
📦Checkpoint 3 — Bulk Registration
  • Quantity-driven loop — each cylinder gets unique serial number; no batch/shared serial
  • All-or-nothing behaviour — if any registration fails, already-registered cylinders retain records (sequential, not transactional); partial results inspectable

Scenario 4 — Bulk Gas Tank Management

4 Checkpoints
🏭Checkpoint 1 — Tank Creation
  • Must be at FILLING_PLANT or DEPOT warehouse — sales warehouse assignment blocked
  • Capacity required — maximum capacity (kg) must be provided
  • Initial level cannot exceed capacity — opening level ≤ maximum capacity
  • Status defaults to ACTIVE — new tanks active and ready to receive gas
⚖️Checkpoint 2 — Tank Level Adjustments
  • Positive or negative adjustments supported — upward (receipt) and downward (evaporation, calibration, discrepancy)
  • Level cannot go negative — adjustment below 0 kg rejected
  • Level cannot exceed capacity — adjustment above max capacity rejected
  • All adjustments logged — BulkGasTankTransaction created with type, amount, before/after levels, user
⬇️Checkpoint 3 — Automatic Level Deduction (Filling)
  • On Filling Session completion, total net gas filled auto-deducted from source tank — no manual entry needed
  • Tank transaction log records deduction as FILLING type
📜Checkpoint 4 — Transaction History
  • Paginated, chronological log of every level-changing event available via endpoint
  • Covers: gas receipts, filling session deductions, manual calibration adjustments, loss/shrinkage write-offs
  • Complete chain-of-custody for every kilogram of gas

Scenario 5 — Cylinder Filling Flow

5 Checkpoints
🆕Checkpoint 1 — Session Creation
  • Warehouse must be FILLING_PLANT type — regular warehouses, depots, distribution points rejected
  • Tank must be at same warehouse — source tank physically located at session warehouse
  • Tank must be ACTIVE — inactive or decommissioned tanks rejected as filling source
📲Checkpoint 2 — Adding Cylinders to Session
  • Session must be OPEN or IN_PROGRESS — completed or cancelled sessions rejected
  • Cylinder must be at filling plant — cross-depot or customer-held cylinders blocked
  • Cylinder must NOT be FULL — full cylinders blocked to prevent double-counting
  • Cylinder must NOT be NEEDS_INSPECTION — flagged cylinders blocked from fill queue until inspection passed
  • Cylinder must NOT be SCRAPPED — condemned cylinders cannot be filled
  • No duplicates in session — same cylinder scanned twice blocked with conflict error
  • Must be a tracked cylinder — only serial numbers designated as cylinder records allowed (not bulk product stock items)
  • On first successful scan, session auto-transitions from OPEN to IN_PROGRESS and records start time
⚖️Checkpoint 3 — Recording Fill Results
  • Item must be PENDING — already FILLED or REJECTED cylinders cannot be processed again
  • Gross weight required — actual post-fill gross weight (kg) must be provided
  • Net gas calculated automatically — net = gross − tare weight; stored tare used automatically, operator can override
  • Cylinder marked FULL after fill — fillStatus → FULL, grossWeight and lastRefillDate updated, fill count increments by 1
❌Checkpoint 4 — Rejecting a Cylinder
  • Item must be PENDING — only unfilled cylinders can be rejected
  • Fill status set to NEEDS_INSPECTION — cylinder flagged requiring inspection before re-entering fill queue
  • Effectively quarantined from fill queue until cleared
🏁Checkpoint 5 — Completing the Session
  • All items must be FILLED or REJECTED — session cannot complete if any cylinder still PENDING
  • Tank deducted automatically — total net gas across all FILLED cylinders deducted atomically from source tank
  • Tank level cannot go negative — completion blocked if total gas filled would take tank below zero
  • Cannot cancel session with FILLED cylinders — must be completed if at least one fill completed

Scenario 6 — Cylinder Tracking Functionality

7 Checkpoints
📱Checkpoint 1 — Real-Time QR Code Scanning
  • Any authorised device can scan cylinder QR code to retrieve live status
  • Returns: current status (IN_STOCK / IN_TRANSIT / SOLD / SCRAPPED)
  • Returns: fill status (FULL / EMPTY / PARTIAL / NEEDS_INSPECTION)
  • Returns: current warehouse location (or null if with customer)
  • Returns: current customer (if sold), last fill date, fill count, gross/tare weight, manufacture date, last test date
📍Checkpoint 2 — Location Integrity
  • Warehouse-bound scanning — cylinder can only scan onto filling session or dispatch trip if at correct warehouse; warehouseId cross-checked
  • No phantom movements — every transition generates stock movement record (stock-out on dispatch departure, stock-in on return)
  • Customer location tracking — on delivery warehouseId cleared and customerId set; on empty return enters IN_TRANSIT until back at warehouse
⚠️Checkpoint 3 — Weight Discrepancy Detection
  • Cylinder returned as PARTIAL — fill status set to PARTIAL, gross weight recorded; cannot be directly dispatched until refilled
  • Discrepancy recorded — WeightDiscrepancy record created linking cylinder, trip, and measured weight
  • Push notification sent — mobile alert (⚠️ Weight Discrepancy Alert) to responsible users with cylinder serials and trip number
  • Trip completion context — discrepancies reviewed as part of trip completion; outstanding discrepancies surfaced in completion summary
🔍Checkpoint 4 — Cylinder Inspection Lifecycle
  • Inspection pass — sets next inspection due date (based on configured hydrostatic test interval); clears NEEDS_INSPECTION flag
  • Inspection fail — blocks cylinder from dispatch and filling; status remains NEEDS_INSPECTION until re-inspected
  • Overdue inspection — past-due cylinders surfaced in cylinder dashboard under "Needs Inspection" statistics
🗑️Checkpoint 5 — Condemnation (Scrapping)
  • Can only condemn IN_STOCK cylinders — IN_TRANSIT or SOLD cylinders blocked while outside warehouse
  • Reason required — condemnation reason must be recorded
  • Permanent — scrapped cylinders cannot be re-activated; remain for historical reporting but excluded from operational counts
📊Checkpoint 6 — Cylinder Dashboard Metrics
  • Total cylinders — all registered, non-scrapped cylinders
  • By fill status — count of FULL / EMPTY / PARTIAL / NEEDS_INSPECTION
  • By operational status — count of IN_STOCK / IN_TRANSIT / SOLD / SCRAPPED
  • Needs inspection — count of cylinders past inspection due date
  • Ready for dispatch — cylinders that are IN_STOCK + FULL
  • By location — filter cylinder list by warehouse showing all cylinders at specific location
👤Checkpoint 7 — Customer Cylinder Accountability
  • Customer ID recorded on serial number record upon delivery
  • Delivery reference and sale date recorded
  • Cylinder can only be collected as empty return from same customer it was delivered to — cross-customer swaps blocked
  • If cylinder has no registered customer ID but is in SOLD status, collection blocked and data integrity issue flagged

Scenario 7 — Bulk Gas Batch: Receipt → Dual Fill → Deplete (Role-Aware)

10 Checkpoints

📖 The Story — read this before you start testing

Tuesday, 06:40. A road tanker pulls into the plant carrying roughly 33 tonnes of LPG that Future Gaz has already committed to buy. It rolls onto the weigh-bridge loaded, offloads into the big storage tank, and rolls back on empty. The difference between those two weights is the gas — and it is the only honest number in the whole transaction. Everything downstream is built on it.

That gas now sits in the tank as a batch: one lot the plant will work through until it runs dry. Over the next few days it gets pulled in two completely different directions. Some of it goes into a bobtail tanker — a truck that drives bulk gas out to customers and pumps it into their tanks on site. The rest goes onto the filling line, packed into 50 kg cylinders that go out on delivery trips. Same tank, same batch, two different businesses drinking from it.

Eventually the tank runs dry and someone walks out with a torch, reads the gauge on the side of it, and says: that's it, it's finished. That moment is the deplete, and it is the moment of truth. The system knows what it received and what it says it dispensed. The gauge knows what is physically left. Subtract one from the other and you get the number nobody wants to discuss: how much gas went missing.

Why this scenario is built the way it is. LPG is easy to lose and easy to steal, and every loss looks like an honest measurement error until proven otherwise. So the controls are not really about arithmetic — they are about making sure no single person can both move gas and sign off on the movement. That is why you will be logging in and out constantly. The role switching is not demo choreography. It is the thing being tested.

Dispatcher — runs the plant's paperwork. Keys the weigh-bridge figures, logs the daily gauge readings, checks the driver's refill, and closes the batch at the end. Cannot accept a delivery. Must also be assigned the "Bulk Gas Operator" role — see the warning below.
Station Manager — the accepting authority. Says yes to the delivery and yes to letting a bobtail take gas. Signs off; does not operate. Must also be assigned the "Bulk Gas Approver" role.
Driver — asks for gas for their tanker and records what physically went in. Sees their own truck and nothing else.
Accountant — the commercial gate. Approves the customer orders (bobtail and 50 kg) that this gas is sold against, and sees the ledger. Never touches the plant.
Bulk Gas Operator / Approver — optional split, if the business wants bulk gas delegated away from the dispatcher/manager. Operator does, Approver signs.
⚠️ Before you begin — do not run this scenario as a tenant-admin. Admin accounts hold wildcard permissions and can do everything, which makes every gate below invisible and the whole scenario worthless. You need five separate logins, and you will switch between them roughly a dozen times. Have them all ready — ideally on separate devices, or at minimum with fast logout/login — before the stakeholders sit down.
⚠️ Setup — confirm the permission fix has been deployed. Reaching the Bulk Gas area needs two things: permission to use the feature, and permission to navigate to it (the Inventory tab must appear at all). Until recently the base Dispatcher and Station Manager roles had the first but not the second, so the hub was unreachable on those roles and only an admin could raise a receipt. A fix now grants both roles the navigation permission plus the three lookups the receipt form needs (tanks, suppliers, purchase orders). Check the fix is present in the environment you are testing: log in as the plain Dispatcher and confirm the Inventory tab appears. If it does not, stop — the environment predates the fix and Checkpoints 2–4 will fail for configuration reasons, not product ones.

🚛Checkpoint 5 — Bobtail Refill Drawn from the Batch
🔄 Four role switches — in this exact order 1. Driver 2. Station Manager 3. Driver 4. Dispatcher This is the most role-heavy checkpoint in the guide and the order is not negotiable — the session physically cannot advance out of turn. Driver requests the refill → Station Manager approves it → Driver (log back in) records what went into the truck → Dispatcher verifies it independently. Four hands, three people, one tanker of gas. As you switch, check each account for buttons that should not be there: the Driver must never see Approve or Verify.
  • Driver requests a refill against the batch's source tank; session opens as REQUESTED with number BFS-YYYY-NNNN. Source tank must be ACTIVE or the request is refused.
    Role: Driver — step 1 of 4
  • Only one active refill session per tanker — a second request while one is live is refused and names the existing session number.
    Role: Driver
  • Station Manager approves the request → APPROVED. The Driver's own screen for the same session shows no Approve button. The tank is re-validated at approval in case it went out of service since the request.
    Role: Station Manager — step 2 of 4
  • Driver records the fill, choosing the measurement method once: rota gauge (%) via the tanker's calibration chart, or onboard flow-meter (litres). The method is fixed at this moment for the rest of the session.
    Role: Driver — step 3 of 4
  • Closing reading must exceed opening — a fill raises the level. Try to record a closing below opening and confirm it is refused in plain language.
    Role: Driver — negative test
  • Tanker capacity is checked before any gas moves — a reading that would put more on the tanker than it holds is refused with the tanker's registration and its capacity named, and the tank is not debited.
    Role: Driver — negative test
  • Overfill past the 85% safe limit warns and flags but never blocks — the physical reading reflects reality and the system records it. Confirm the flag is visible to the Station Manager and Dispatcher, not just stored.
    Role: Driver records → Station Manager / Dispatcher observe
  • A session cannot be filled twice — a second record-fill on the same session is refused and quotes the quantity already recorded.
    Role: Driver — negative test
  • Dispatcher verifies the fill — and is forced to re-read using whichever method the Driver used. A gauge-filled session demands gauge percentages; a meter-filled session demands litres. The verifier cannot switch methods.
    Role: Dispatcher — step 4 of 4. Driver has no Verify button.
  • Regression test — enter a deliberately wrong verification figure. When the Dispatcher's reading disagrees with the Driver's fill by more than 50 kg, the session is flagged and the tanker's books are left where the Driver's fill put them. A flagged reading is by definition untrusted, so the system must not settle stock on it. Previously a single mis-typed number wrote off ~1,985 kg of real gas this way.
    Role: Dispatcher — the highest-value test in this scenario

  • Loss/gain is computed as (opening stock + received − final gauge) − total filled. Work it through on paper with the demo's own figures before revealing the system's number — a positive result is a loss, negative is a gain. Stakeholders should see the arithmetic, not just trust it.

  • The tank's system level is reconciled to the final physical gauge, writing a visible loss/adjustment transaction against the tank — the gap between books and reality is recorded, never quietly absorbed.
    Role: Dispatcher
  • Only an OPEN batch can be depleted — attempt to deplete the same batch twice and confirm the second attempt is refused by name and status rather than double-counting the loss.
    Role: Dispatcher — negative test
  • Raise with stakeholders: declaring deplete needs only the create permission, not approve. The Dispatcher or Bulk Gas Operator closes the batch and books the loss unilaterally — no second signature, unlike the receipt that opened it. Confirm the business accepts a one-person close on the number that becomes the loss figure.
    Role: Dispatcher — governance gap to confirm or close
  • Confirm the next receipt after deplete opens a fresh batch, with its opening stock set to the remainder the deplete left behind — closing the loop back to Checkpoint 4.
    Role: Dispatcher creates → Station Manager confirms

Summary of Key Business Rules (Non-Bypassable)

  • Empty cylinders cannot be dispatched. Only FULL cylinders can be scanned onto a trip.
  • Cylinders from wrong locations cannot be loaded. A cylinder must physically be at the trip's source warehouse.
  • Vehicle capacity cannot be exceeded. The number of scanned cylinders cannot exceed the vehicle's registered capacity.
  • All ordered cylinders must be scanned before departure. The truck cannot depart until every expected cylinder is accounted for.
  • Empty returns must belong to the delivering customer. Cross-customer return collection is prevented at the technical level.
  • Cylinders needing inspection cannot be filled. The NEEDS_INSPECTION flag blocks a cylinder from re-entering the fill queue.
  • Filling sessions cannot complete if any cylinder result is outstanding. Every cylinder must be either filled or rejected.
  • Payment amounts cannot exceed outstanding balances. Overpayments are rejected.
  • Split payment totals must exactly equal the transaction amount. Rounding differences beyond 0.01 are rejected.
  • Dispatch orders on trips cannot be directly completed. The trip completion workflow must be used to ensure all cylinders are accounted for.
  • Being allowed to use a feature is not the same as being able to reach it. Navigation is a separate permission per module, and without it the whole tab is hidden — so a role can hold a feature's permissions and still have no route to them. Check both when granting access.
  • Bulk gas cannot be received without a Purchase Order. Every offload traces back to the order that authorised it.
  • The person who keys the weigh-bridge figures cannot accept them. Receipt entry and receipt confirmation are separate permissions held by different roles.
  • Received quantity is derived, not typed. Net weight (gross − tare) is what enters the tank.
  • One open batch per tank. A receipt confirmed while a batch is open tops that batch up; it cannot open a second.
  • A bobtail tanker cannot be filled twice on one session, cannot exceed its capacity, and cannot be filled from a tank that is out of service.
  • A bobtail refill is verified by someone other than the driver who filled it, re-reading by the same method the driver used.
  • A disputed verification never settles the books. Where the verifier's reading disagrees with the driver's fill beyond tolerance, the session is flagged and stock is left untouched.
  • Only an open batch can be depleted. A batch cannot be closed twice, so a loss cannot be double-counted.
  • Deplete reconciles the tank to the physical gauge and records the gap as a visible loss or gain. The difference between books and reality is never silently absorbed.
