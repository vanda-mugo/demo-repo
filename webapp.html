How to use this suite

Work module by module against the live web app. Tick each check that passes and use the notes box to record evidence or a defect reference. Progress and notes save automatically in this browser. Test with a normal operational role, not an administrator — admin hides every access gate. Business rules themselves are covered in the mobile stakeholder checklist (Scenarios 1–8); this suite confirms the web client surfaces and enforces them.

1. Access, Login & Role-Based Navigation

3 areas
🔑Login & session
  • Valid credentials log in and land on the Dashboard; the tenant name shows in the header.
  • Invalid credentials are rejected with a clear message (no crash, no blank page).
  • A page refresh keeps the user signed in (session/token persists); it does not force re-login.
  • Logout clears the session and returns to the login page; the back button cannot re-enter the app.
🧭Role-based navigation
  • The left navigation shows ONLY the modules the signed-in role may use (e.g. an accountant sees Accounting, not Dispatch).
  • Signing in as a limited role (e.g. Sales Rep) shows fewer menu items than an admin — capture both for contrast.
  • Typing a forbidden route directly in the URL bar (e.g. /accounting/journal-entries as a driver) is blocked or redirected — not shown.
  • Actions the role cannot perform are absent, not shown-then-error (no dead buttons).
🖥️Browser & layout
  • Loads and works in Chrome/Edge and Firefox with no console errors on the main screens.
  • Usable at laptop width and tablet width; wide tables scroll inside their panel rather than breaking the page.
  • Branding is consistent (Future Gaz green theme, logo) across every module.

2. Master Data

3 areas
👥Customers
  • Customers list loads with working search and pagination.
  • Create a customer — required-field validation fires; credit limit & terms can be set.
  • Edit an existing customer and confirm the change persists after reload.
  • Open a customer detail — balance, credit and contact info display.
🏭Suppliers, Categories, Units
  • Suppliers: list, create (with validation), edit, and view detail.
  • Product Categories: list, create, edit — including the revenue/COGS account codes used by accounting.
  • Units of measure: list, create, edit.
📍Warehouses & Tax Rates
  • Warehouses: list, create, edit; the warehouse TYPE (e.g. Filling Plant / Depot) can be chosen.
  • Tax Rates: list, create, edit; effective-from/‑to dates apply.
  • A tax rate shows the correct status chip — Active, Pending (future), or Expired (past).

3. Inventory — Products & Stock

3 areas
🧴Products
  • Products list loads with search; the 6/13/50 kg cylinder and Bulk LPG products are present.
  • Create a product with category, unit and account codes; stock/serial-tracking flags can be set.
  • Edit a product; open a product detail view.
📊Stock levels & movements
  • Stock Levels show on-hand, reserved and available per warehouse.
  • Stock Movements list loads, filters by type/warehouse, and a movement detail opens.
🔧Adjustments & transfers
  • Create an inventory adjustment (increase/decrease/stocktake) with a reason; it posts and appears in the list.
  • The adjustment produces a GL journal entry (cross-check in Accounting).
  • Create a stock transfer between warehouses; the source shows the stock leaving (in-transit).
  • Receive the transfer at the destination; destination stock increases and the transfer closes.

4. Inventory — 50 kg Cylinders

2 areas
🆕Registration
  • Register a new 50 kg cylinder; the system serial is generated in the NFG50KGS-YYMM-NNNN format.
  • Embedded/manufacturer serials and tare weight can be captured at registration.
  • Registering a cylinder increases the 50 kg stock level (stock sync).
🔎List, detail & locations
  • Cylinder list loads with search; status (IN_STOCK / IN_TRANSIT / SOLD / SCRAPPED) and fill status are shown.
  • A cylinder detail shows its history — fills, movements, current location/customer.
  • Cylinder Locations view shows where cylinders currently are.

5. Inventory — Bulk Gas

3 areas
🫙Tanks
  • Create a bulk gas tank with capacity; the tank list shows current level.
  • A tank detail shows its transaction history (receipts, dispenses, adjustments).
⚖️Weigh-bridge receipts
  • Create a receipt against a Purchase Order with gross & tare weigh-bridge readings; net = gross − tare is derived (not typed).
  • A draft receipt can be edited/cancelled; a confirmed receipt cannot be edited.
  • Confirming a receipt raises the tank level and creates a GRNI journal entry (cross-check in Accounting).
  • The person entering the receipt cannot also confirm it unless their role allows — the Confirm action reflects the role.
📉Rota gauge & batches
  • Log a daily rota-gauge reading; the variance vs the system level is shown.
  • Confirming the first receipt after a deplete opens a batch (BGB-YYYY-NNNN); a second receipt tops it up (one open batch per tank).
  • Declare Deplete with a final gauge reading; loss/gain is computed and the tank reconciles to the gauge.

6. Sales

2 areas
➕Create order
  • Create a sales order for each type — Field (50 kg), Counter, Bobtail; the product picker only offers products valid for that type.
  • Order totals (subtotal, tax, shipping, discount) calculate correctly.
  • Payment-type rules apply — FULL needs a bank account; PARTIAL needs account + amount; CREDIT needs none.
✅Approvals & invoices
  • The approvals queue lists SUBMITTED orders; only submitted orders can be approved or rejected.
  • Rejecting requires a reason, which is stored against the order.
  • The Customer Debtor Report is reachable from approval — credit limit, outstanding, aging buckets, unpaid invoices.
  • Invoices list and an invoice detail open and link back to the order.

7. Orders

1 areas
🔄Order lifecycle
  • The Orders list loads with status filters; an order detail shows line items and history.
  • The status reflects its stage (DRAFT → SUBMITTED → CONFIRMED → PROCESSING → DELIVERED / COMPLETED).
  • The order shows its Accounting status chip (Pending / Posted / Failed) once delivered.

8. Dispatch

3 areas
🚐Vehicles
  • Vehicles list, create and edit; a vehicle type (standard truck / bobtail tanker) and capacity can be set.
  • A bobtail tanker's capacity is fixed by the calibration chart (5,415 kg) and is not freely editable.
🗺️Dispatch orders & trips
  • Create a dispatch order from a CONFIRMED 50 kg sales order; non-50 kg products are rejected/redirected.
  • Create a trip, assign dispatch orders, and move it through loading → depart → deliver → complete.
  • Cylinder scanning onto a trip enforces the rules (FULL only, correct warehouse, capacity, no duplicates).
📛Bobtail & discrepancies
  • Create a bobtail delivery trip from a confirmed bobtail order; move it dispatch → deliver → gate-in → close with meter readings.
  • Delivering a bobtail/50 kg trip recognises revenue (cross-check the journal entries in Accounting).
  • Weight Discrepancies and Bobtail Variances lists load and an entry can be reviewed/resolved.

9. Accounting

4 areas
📚Chart of accounts & ledger
  • The Chart of Accounts loads; opening an account drills into its General Ledger of transactions.
  • Bank Accounts list and detail open; each carries an account type (cash / mobile money / bank).
💳Customer payments
  • Record a customer payment; the method must match the receiving account type (e.g. mobile money into the M-Pesa account, not a cash till).
  • A payment can be allocated against outstanding invoices; the customer balance reduces.
🧮Journal entries
  • The Journal Entries list loads; every entry is balanced (debits = credits).
  • A 50 kg delivery produces a COGS entry (DR COGS / CR Inventory) AND an AR entry (DR AR / CR Revenue + VAT).
  • A bobtail delivery produces COGS (at the tanker's blended cost) and AR — with COGS booked once, not twice.
📈Financial reports
  • Trial Balance loads and balances (total debits = total credits).
  • Balance Sheet and Income Statement render with sensible figures.
  • AR Aging and AP Aging reports load and bucket balances by age.

10. Cross-Cutting & Data Integrity

3 areas
🔁Web ↔ mobile parity
  • A record created on the web app (e.g. a customer or order) appears on the mobile app, and vice-versa — same backend, one source of truth.
  • Status changes made on one client are reflected on the other after refresh.
🛡️Security & resilience
  • A forbidden action attempted via direct URL or the browser dev-tools/API is refused by the server (403), not merely hidden in the UI.
  • Validation errors are shown clearly next to the field or in a banner; nothing fails silently.
  • A dropped network / server error surfaces a readable message and the app recovers on retry.
✨Polish
  • Long lists paginate or virtualise; there are no broken links or dead-end pages.
  • No uncaught errors appear in the browser console during a normal walkthrough of each module.
